What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science that focuses on creating systems that can perform tasks that usually require human intelligence.

AI systems can learn from data, recognize patterns, understand language, and make decisions. Machine Learning and Deep Learning are important areas within Artificial Intelligence.

  • Deep Learning

  • Computer Vision
